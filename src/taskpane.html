Chart

Paste from Excel: copy a range with series names down the left and categories across the top, click the first data cell above, and paste. The chart updates as you type.

Timeline:

Dates: 15/03/2026, 15 Mar 2026, Mar 2026, Q1 2027 or 2027 all work. Paste from Excel as Label | Start | End | Note into the first Label cell: rows without dates become groups, rows with only a start become milestones.
